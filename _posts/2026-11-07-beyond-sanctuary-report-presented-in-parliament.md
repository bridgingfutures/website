On **3 November 2026**, the University of Birmingham launched its *Beyond Sanctuary: Building the Future of Ukrainians in the UK* report in Parliament. The event was organised by the university's Ukraine Policy Network and Ukrainian Voice UK, and hosted by **Sir Roger Gale MP**.

According to the university, around **70 people** attended, including MPs, members of the House of Lords, researchers and community representatives. Bridging Futures was pleased to be among the participants.

Drawing on almost **5,500 survey responses** and interviews with practitioners, the report examines the effects of prolonged temporary immigration status on the lives and prospects of displaced Ukrainians in the UK.

Its key recommendation is a **clear pathway to settlement**, alongside recognition of time already spent under the Ukraine Schemes and Ukraine Permission Extension (UPE) towards existing routes, including **Long Residence and Private Life**.

The parliamentary launch brought renewed attention to a question that remains unresolved: what long-term future will be available to Ukrainians who have built their lives in the UK?

**Source:** University of Birmingham, 5 November 2026. [Read the university's report on the parliamentary launch](https://blog.bham.ac.uk/supportukrainians/2026/11/05/from-temporary-protection-to-long-term-futures-new-evidence-from-ukrainians-in-the-uk/).
